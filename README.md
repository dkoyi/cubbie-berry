# cubbie-berry

Team wildberry, DevHacks 2026.

## Track 1 Cubbie Skill: `group-chat-assistant`

[`group-chat-assistant/SKILL.md`](group-chat-assistant/SKILL.md) turns a student's own course material into a text quiz in iMessage. It finds their weak topics, explains them, and can drill those topics nightly until the exam. It can also make a study guide the student shares with their study group chat.

**Install:** text Cubbie the link to this folder:

```
https://github.com/dkoyi/cubbie-berry/tree/main/group-chat-assistant
```

**Cubbie abilities used:** Canvas (connected), files and media, memory, reminders and scheduled check-ins (including the wake-up call), and documents.
**Sign-in:** approach 2 (Canvas, already connected to Cubbie). No scripts, keys, or `cubbie.yaml` needed.

**Test it:** after installing, text "quiz me on <class> for my midterm" or send a photo of your notes.
